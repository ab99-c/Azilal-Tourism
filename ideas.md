# أفكار تصميم موقع السياحة في أزيلال

## النهج المختار: "البربري المعاصر" — Contemporary Berber

### Style Decisions
- Every major section includes a visible Amazigh/Tifinagh geometric motif as structural element
- Page composition avoids repeated centered template sections; alternates asymmetric blocks
- Imagery focuses on Moroccan/Azilal: High Atlas landscapes, red earth, waterfalls, Amazigh villages
- Arabic copy carries poetic local invitation tone: "أصيل، طبيعي، ضيافة"
- Logo merges Yaz ⵣ symbol with Atlas mountain shape
- Multilingual (AR/EN/FR/BER) with dynamic RTL/LTR switching
